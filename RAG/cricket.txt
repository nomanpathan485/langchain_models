**The Spirit of Cricket**

The morning sun lights up the ground,  
As eager players gather round.  
The pitch lies ready, wickets stand,  
A polished ball rests in a hand.  

The bowler runs with steady pace,  
Determination on his face.  
The batter watches, standing tall,  
Then lifts his bat to meet the ball.  

A crack rings out across the green,  
The finest shot the crowd has seen.  
It races past a diving hand,  
And cheers erupt from every stand.  

Now fielders shift and captains call,  
Preparing for the coming ball.  
A spinner gives a clever twist,  
The batter swings—the ball is missed!  

The keeper whips the bails away,  
Appeals ring out across the play.  
The umpire’s finger rises high,  
A happy roar climbs to the sky.  

Yet cricket lives beyond the score,  
In dusty lanes and so much more.  
A wooden plank becomes a bat,  
A wicket stands where bricks are sat.  

With friends beside us, young and old,  
We play until the dusk turns gold.  
No grand pavilion, shining cup,  
Just one more game before we pack up.  

The final over, six to win,  
The bowler takes a breath again.  
The batter swings; we hold our breath,  
The ball sails high above the rest.  

Beyond the rope! The match is won,  
The players hug beneath the sun.  
And when the cheering fades away,  
We dream of cricket’s next great day.
